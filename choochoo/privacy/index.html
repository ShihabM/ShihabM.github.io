Privacy Policy

Choochoo was built independently by Shihab Mehboob.

Choochoo saves your game progress and preferences on your device. The app has no advertising or analytics tools, and the developer does not receive your saved game data. You can remove the locally saved data by deleting the app from your device.

If you’re signed in to Apple’s Game Center, Choochoo reports achievement progress to Game Center. You can manage activity sharing in your device’s Game Center settings. See Apple’s Game Center privacy notice for details.

The Choochoo website does not use cookies or analytics and does not ask you to provide personal information.
